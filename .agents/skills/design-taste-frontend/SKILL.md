# Design Taste Frontend

Apply strong art direction before implementation. Infer the product's design language from its purpose and existing interface instead of defaulting to generic SaaS patterns.

## Core rules

- Establish hierarchy, grid, spacing rhythm and typography before decorative effects.
- Avoid repetitive card grids, unnecessary containers, generic gradients and interchangeable AI-generated layouts.
- Keep motion purposeful and tied to state or hierarchy.
- Prefer coherent reusable tokens/components over one-off styling.
- Preserve usability, accessibility and responsiveness while increasing visual quality.

## Gasto Certo direction

For Gasto Certo, optimize for financial confidence and quick comprehension. Important numbers and actions must dominate decoration. Use expressive design selectively for onboarding, insights and success moments, while transaction entry and financial dashboards remain calm and precise.
